Hi, my name is Nathan Elliott. I'm a 1st year student in IMD at Algonquin College. I have previously graduated from the art stream of the Game Development program. My dream has always been to make a game of my own, however I can also see myself in the graphic design industry. I always strive to create art that is enjoyable and functional.
